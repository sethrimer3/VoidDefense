# Void Defense — Domain Glossary

Domain terms used across code, UI, and documentation.

## Core Concepts

- **Void**: The violet cosmic breach that expands outward across the world lattice, eroding terrain and collapsing buildings.
- **Frontier**: The active boundary of non-Void cells adjacent to Void cells.
- **Perimeter Budget**: Fixed-point erosion energy distributed each tick across the Void frontier; lengthening the frontier with sacrificial islands dilutes pressure per cell.
- **Supplies**: In-run tactical currency generated by surviving civilian buildings (with diminishing returns over time) and spent to place/upgrade towers and cast strikes.
- **Delivery Depot**: A player-built structure that sends delivery cars to its furthest depot (2 and then 3 furthest when upgraded) and earns Supplies from completed deliveries.
- **Depot Link**: The connection from a Delivery Depot to one of its target depots, shown by a Tron light beam that runs along the street route to the target and back, then settles into a faint trace.
- **Street Fire**: A persistent fire in a trash can, debris pile or trash heap that burns all level until something destroys it.
- **Void Bed**: The continuous, evolving procedural ambient soundscape of the Void (rumble, mid-low body, airy cosmic wind, harmonic pad, and distant earthquake swells) whose stereo width and pan scale with the Void's size and horizontal extent.
- **Void Essence**: Persistent between-run currency earned from resisting Void work and preserving the city; spent in the Global Tech Network.
- **Sparks of Defiance**: Milestone currency (0–3 per sector, shared across modes) awarded for winning at ≥35% (Pyrrhic Containment), ≥55% (Breach Contained), and ≥90% (Flawless Defense) preserved.
- **Sector**: One of 7 campaign maps (Sector 01–07) or Endless: Event Horizon.
- **Build Under Siege**: Real-time mode where players build, upgrade, and cast strikes while the Void expands.
- **Build & Watch**: Planning mode where players spend a fixed starting budget during prep and then watch their defense run.
- **Coach**: The 4-step guided onboarding callout shown on Sector 01 for new players.
- **Rooftop Turret**: A turret mounted on a building's roof. It gains range from the height and crashes to the street, damaged, if the roof under it gives way.
- **Street-Level Structure**: A structure that fields units, runs deliveries or works the ground (barracks, bays, depots, posts, mines); it is always built on streets, sidewalks, plazas or open ground, never on a building.

## Towers (16)

- **Matter Cannon**: Fires synthetic land pellets that displace Void and reinforce crumbling frontiers.
- **Repair Depot**: Deploys 1×2 engineers to haul salvage, repair skyscrapers, and upgrade city income.
- **Infantry Barracks**: Deploys 1-pixel riflemen that destroy Void Nodes, organisms, and ablate frontier pressure.
- **Constructor Hub**: Deploys autonomous masons that build player-drawn wall blueprints.
- **Barrier Projector**: Projects an energy shield wall that slows Void erosion.
- **Wall Serpent Bay**: Launches an articulated armored serpent that patrols and hardens frontier terrain.
- **Gravity Anchor**: Emits a gravity pulse that pulls debris into reinforced ground and slows cosmic organisms.
- **Rocket Battery**: Long-range salvo battery firing matter warheads at distant breaches.
- **Heavy Mech Bay**: Deploys a heavy armored exo-mech that clears Void Nodes and hunts major organisms.
- **Void Siphon**: Absorbs frontier Void pressure into a storage tank and vents it as reclaimed ground.
- **Boundary Prism**: Projects an angled optical plane that redirects Void erosion pressure along its axis.
- **Chrono Cannon**: Records local terrain and units, rewinding a Void breach back to its earlier state.
- **Beacon Drone Post**: Deploys an aerial drone whose ground halo stabilizes terrain against Void collapse.
- **Seamwalker Post**: Deploys a swift frontier patroller that tracks the Void rim and aborts nearby Void Nodes.
- **Echo Mine**: Kinetic mine that detonates in a matter-restoring shockwave when triggered by Void contact.
- **Countervoid Heart**: Generates a steady anti-Void pulse that reclaims nearby Void cells into solid ground.

## Strikes (6)

- **Solar Anchor**: Drops a reinforced land island onto the Void to split its perimeter budget.
- **Stasis Beacon**: Temporarily seals a circular boundary, halting Void crossing.
- **Containment Pulse**: Emits a shockwave pushing back Void pressure and damaging nearby organisms.
- **Genesis Wave**: Terraforms a large reclaimed disc ringed with ferrocrete.
- **Orbital Drop Squad**: Deploys emergency infantry directly to a target sector.
- **Seismic Stiffener**: Reinforces buildings and terrain within range against spalling and erosion.

## Cosmic Organisms

- **Void Node**: Crystalline spire near the frontier that boosts local Void erosion until destroyed.
- **Nebula Worm**, **Constellation Spider**, **Void Bloom**, **Nebula Queen**, **Void Bee**, **Honeycomb Crystal**, **Nexus Crystal**: Reservoir-funded cosmic organisms that attack defenses or spawn Void hazards.

## Meta Tabs

- **Global Tech Network**: 63-node cross-linked technology DAG across 6 sectors with free between-run refunds.
- **Resistance Ops**: Cosmetic engineering bay and containment lab reflecting unlocked tech and earned Sparks.
- **Resistance Map**: Campaign sector map showing containment trails, sector bests, and deployment controls.
